Features

Nothing is faked

Everything comes from the official Microsoft and Mojang APIs, or from your own disk.

⬇

Real downloads

919 versions from the Mojang manifest, downloaded in parallel, SHA-1 verified, resumable, pausable and cancellable.

🗂

Real saves

Reads level.dat: real world name, game mode, last played date, thumbnail. Import and export as .zip.

📁

Files

Browse the game folder, delete with confirmation. Any access outside that folder is refused.

🌐

Tested servers

Version, players, MOTD and latency measured live via handshake — not a pre-filled list.

🎨

Skin and profile

Your official Minecraft skin drawn in pixel art, your name and UUID, read from the Mojang servers.

✦

XRN cosmetics

The mod ships with the launcher: the XRN cape is free for everyone, and other capes are earned with the stars the launcher gives you each day.

◈

Cosmetics across players

Your XRN logo shows next to your name — on the name tag, in the player list (Tab) — and the cape you wear is visible to other launcher users, on any server.

🔒

Nothing to buy, nothing leaves

No purchases, no ads. Stars are earned here, day after day. Your token stays in %APPDATA% and is never sent anywhere.

Getting started

Installation

Three steps, under a minute.

Unzip the .zip file

For example on your Desktop. Keep the whole folder: the app needs its files next to it.

XRNLauncher.exe

Double-click it. SmartScreen may object: click More info, then Run anyway. The app is not signed.

Create your profile and play

Login tab, pick a name, then Start. Then Versions, install, and hit PLAY.

⚠ About online login
The Microsoft tab relies on an application registered with Mojang that is currently awaiting approval. Until that reply arrives, online login is not available yet and the launcher runs in solo / LAN mode, with no account to create. Downloads, saves, files and servers all work normally.
Help

FAQ

The game won't start

Nine times out of ten it's Java. Settings tab: the launcher offers to download the right JDK.

“Java not found”

Install Temurin 17 or 21, or set the path in Settings.

A server shows “offline”

Firewall, server down, or a proxy blocking port 25565. Test another server to isolate it.

Blank window

An antivirus is blocking nw.dll. Allow the application folder.
